# DCF E2E Company Coverage

This is a targeted regression list for the general FCFF DCF workflow. It deliberately excludes banks, insurers, and REITs, which need sector-specific valuation structures.

| Priority | Symbol | Why it is useful |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AMZN | Multi-business revenue, significant leases, and complex debt/lease scope. |
| 2 | AAPL | Large cash balance, buybacks, and diluted-share/equity-bridge coverage. |
| 3 | DAL | Aircraft financing and leases; tests debt-versus-lease boundaries. |
| 4 | COST | Membership plus merchandise revenue; tests whether residual revenue is explicitly modeled. |
| 5 | T | High leverage and stable cash flows; tests debt cost and capital-structure weights. |
| 6 | NVDA | Rapid growth and working-capital variation; tests terminal convergence and sensitivities. |
| 7 | MSFT | Cloud segments, substantial cash, and rising infrastructure capex. |
| 8 | GOOGL | Advertising and Cloud segments with high cash reserves. |
| 9 | META | High margins and rapidly changing AI capital expenditure. |
| 10 | XOM | Cyclical cash flow and capital expenditure; tests history normalization. |
| 11 | NFLX | Content assets, amortization, and cash-flow timing differences. |
| 12 | PFE | Patent-expiry revenue decline; tests staged forecast and terminal assumptions. |

Recommended execution order: AMZN → AAPL → DAL → COST → T → NVDA.
